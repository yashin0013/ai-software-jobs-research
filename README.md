# AI and Software Engineering Jobs Research

An evidence-based research project exploring how artificial intelligence is affecting software engineering jobs, hiring, layoffs, productivity, and the skills expected from software engineers.

## Research Question

> **Is AI fundamentally changing the software engineering job market, or are recent layoffs primarily the result of broader economic and industry factors?**

The goal of this project is not to prove a predetermined conclusion.

Instead, it is to examine available evidence and understand what is actually happening in the software engineering job market.

---

## Research Approach

This research uses multiple AI research assistants to gather and analyze information:

* ChatGPT
* Google Gemini

The outputs from both research processes will be compared, cross-checked, and combined.

Important claims and statistics will be traced back to their original sources wherever possible.

### Why use multiple AI systems?

AI research tools can produce incomplete, outdated, or incorrect information.

Using multiple systems allows us to:

* Compare research findings
* Identify disagreements
* Find missing information
* Detect potentially unsupported claims
* Cross-check important statistics
* Build a more reliable final analysis

The AI-generated reports are therefore treated as **research inputs, not as authoritative sources**.

---

## Research Areas

The research focuses on several related questions:

1. How has AI adoption changed software development?
2. What is happening to software engineering employment?
3. How much of the recent tech layoffs can be attributed to AI?
4. What other factors contributed to layoffs after the pandemic hiring boom?
5. Is AI increasing developer productivity?
6. Does increased productivity lead to fewer engineers being needed?
7. Which software engineering tasks are most affected by AI?
8. Which engineering skills are becoming more valuable?
9. How are companies changing their hiring requirements?
10. What could the software engineering job market look like as AI adoption increases?

## Important Principle

This project aims to separate:

**Evidence → Analysis → Interpretation**

from:

**Prediction → Opinion → Hype**

Claims about AI and employment are often presented in extreme terms:

> "AI will replace all developers."

or:

> "AI will have no meaningful impact on software jobs."

The purpose of this research is to examine the evidence between these extremes.
